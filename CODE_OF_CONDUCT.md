# Community conduct

Portico's public spaces exist to help people use and improve the project through useful feedback and respectful discussion.

Participants are expected to be considerate, stay on topic, assume good faith where reasonable, and avoid harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, spam, or the publication of another person's private information.

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community or keep discussions productive. Decisions about project scope and official releases remain with the project owner.

Sensitive conduct concerns should be raised privately through GitHub's reporting tools rather than argued in a public issue.
