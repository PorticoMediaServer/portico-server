## What to deliver
1. **Matching in the manner of Plex.** Today a title without a year never matches and nothing lists what was left behind (B-06.2). Score candidates from the evidence available: title similarity after normalisation (case, punctuation, diacritics, articles, `&`/`and`, roman numerals, transliteration), alternative and original titles from the provider, year (exact, off by one, or absent), kind, runtime against the file's duration when known, and id hints, which settle it outright. Accept the best candidate when confidence is decent, including with a misspelled title or no year. Leave a title unmatched only when there is no confident candidate. Keep evidence, locks, history and undo exactly as they are: they are what make a wrong guess cheap to fix.
2. **A needs-attention list**: every unmatched title and every title matched with low confidence, with the reason and the top candidates, and routes to accept a candidate, search by hand, enter an id, or dismiss. Register the routes on `apikit` and add the `client-core` service and parsers. A client lane builds the screen.
3. **The provider is the owner's choice per library.** A provider interface with a registry; a per-library setting; TMDB the default for video libraries; "local metadata only" (NFO files, embedded tags and local artwork, no network) for every library type. Find what already exists before writing new code: there is TMDB, there are traces of TVDB and AniList work, and music and audiobook sources. Make every provider that exists or half-exists selectable and complete where that is reasonable; a provider that needs the owner's own key takes it as a setting. `Product Contracts/decisions.md` and the Catalogue architecture document disagree about anime; the decision now is TMDB by default with the owner free to choose another.
4. **Pace** (D-21, B-06.11): one provider request and one title a second makes a first import take days. Run as fast as each provider's published limits politely allow, with bounded concurrency, backing off on 429 and 5xx. Take the speed from one shared three-level value (gentle, normal, fast; default normal) through the existing background-pacing helper, so that the owner setting a later lane adds controls it. Review the retry schedule of Q-2 and make it sensible.
5. **Local metadata**: NFO reading is narrow (A-06.23); read the common Kodi and Jellyfin NFO fields, tolerate imperfect files, and release a field's ownership when its NFO is deleted (B-06.15). Local artwork names (`poster.jpg`, `folder.jpg`, `fanart.jpg`, `backdrop`, `logo`, `season01-poster`, `<file>-thumb`).
6. The shared artwork size cap that silently drops covers (B-06.4 / B-10.2) if it is still present: no silent dropping; make the limit a default an owner setting can raise.
7. Every other confirmed bug in document 06 that is still present.
