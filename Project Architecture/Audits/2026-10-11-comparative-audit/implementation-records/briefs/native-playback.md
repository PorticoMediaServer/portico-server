Text subtitles on the native engines honour Portico's caption style and the system's (B-20.12). The offline player uses the subtitles, chapters and markers stored with a download.
7. **Remembered track choices** shown correctly at start, and the track menus reflecting what the server chose.
8. **The remaining player bugs** in document 20 on both platforms (including B-20.5, B-20.19, B-20.21 and the Android HDR switch the android-mpv report describes), fallback fonts so mpv-drawn text in Arabic, Hebrew, Thai, Indic and East Asian scripts is not empty boxes, and the external VobSub question raised in `android-mpv.md`.
