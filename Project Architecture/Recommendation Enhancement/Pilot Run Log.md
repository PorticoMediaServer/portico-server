# Pilot run log

This log records engineering canaries before the 1,000-title G2 pilot. It is not a G2 result.

## 25 September 2026 — Wikidata intake

- A first WDQS film query covering 2010–2019 with label service and global ordering produced no response before a 90-second client timeout. Retries were stopped; query text/hash and failure metadata are frozen under the ignored `.cache/wikidata/canary-movie-2010-2019-middle/` run.
- A simpler direct-film, P577-only, 2018–2019 date-range query with no label service or ordering returned 30 rows / 29 unique QIDs in 11 seconds. Query hash: `707f0039ea7ed4f1fc80437de575d86d044590b1129a9799395d88c2efe335e0`.
- Wikidata Action API `wbgetentities` hydration returned `maxlag` on two bounded attempts. No partial candidate output was accepted.
- Wikidata's documented `Special:EntityData/QID.json` route hydrated the first three QIDs in 19.3 seconds (18 serial requests including reference labels) and then the complete 29-QID canary in another 115.6 seconds (116 new requests and 18 cache hits). No 429/503 occurred. All 29 have a matchable provider ID and English Wikipedia sitelink. The 29-row source pool is frozen in `.cache/wikidata/canary-light-movie-2018-2019-middle/`.
- The query window is **not** a reliable work-year stratum: only 13 of 29 hydrated titles have an earliest recorded year in 2018–2019; the others include later country releases or re-releases of older works (earliest years 1952–2017). Sampling and reporting must use the hydrated canonical work year, and query-year discrepancy must remain visible.

## 25 September 2026 — Wikipedia source and Luna handoff

- Three selected film QIDs (389303, 519594, 520051) had revision-pinned English Wikipedia packets. Source revisions: 1373184823, 1364488271, and 1363540323. Article text was bounded to 5–7k characters each. The entire packet file is 23,605 bytes. All three packet fingerprints passed the strict enrichment validator.
- Their candidate rows and source packets were imported to `.cache/source-canary-3/ledger.sqlite` with provisional `movie` tagging threshold N=10; the source batch is `canary-3-v1`. These three prove the workflow mechanics only. They do not establish global coverage or G2 tag quality.
- The first Luna instruction contract validated and checkpointed all three outputs, but the decisions were not committed to the build ledger. Source review found overtagging: earthquake harm was treated as physical violence, real Lapland as a mythic-world setting, and background production facts as central themes. A corrected pass left two titles tagged and one untagged.
- A blind independent Luna pass had mean tag Jaccard agreement about 0.34 against the corrected pass (three titles; too small for a coverage estimate). This is below the pilot target of 0.6 and blocks larger tagging batches until the instructions are calibrated.
- The first contract also prohibited model knowledge and therefore produced no similar suggestions from these isolated packets. Justin explicitly authorized Luna to decide when supplied sources are insufficient. A versioned second contract is being implemented with private per-tag evidence and an explicit model-knowledge basis. The three-title comparison will be repeated under that contract before scaling.
- The versioned v2 three-title main and blind passes both validated 3/3 against packet fingerprints and source revisions (0 rejected). Per-title tag Jaccard against the blind pass was 0.421, 0.438, and 0.692, mean 0.517. This improved on v1 but remains below the 0.6 pilot target. The main pass made five model-knowledge similar suggestions; the blind pass made none. Neither pass has been committed as approved decisions to the canonical ledger.
- Review of the v2 main pass found some low-value or weakly supported tags: default `style:live-action` on every film, `pacing:slow-burn` inferred from deaths accumulating in the plot, `pacing:deliberate` inferred from unconventional editing, and an audience tag inferred from festival/cult reception. These are concrete instruction/vocabulary calibration cases. A separate ten-title mixed show/anime canary is running to test whether the pattern recurs before the instructions change again.
- To check the actual build path without accepting the tags, the v2 main checkpoint was committed into a **copy** of the three-title ledger. The copied ledger resolved 0 of 5 suggested similar titles because the private canary corpus contains only 29 unrelated film identities. It built a schema-v1 SQLite engineering artifact with 3 tagged works, 0 similar links, and `PRAGMA integrity_check = ok` (110,592 raw bytes). This artifact is local, unreviewed, and not a size-per-work estimate for G2. The canonical ledger still has no committed decisions.

## 25 September 2026 — Additional candidate diversity canaries

- Three bounded, sequential WDQS queries succeeded without 429/503: 30 show QIDs (2023–2024; ~24 seconds), 27 unique anime QIDs from 30 class bindings (2023–2024; ~1 second), and 30 low-sitelink movie QIDs (2018–2019; ~11 seconds). The queries and raw responses are frozen in separate ignored `.cache/wikidata/` runs. A 15-title hydration slice across those groups is next; broad acquisition has not started.
- Hydration of 15 diverse works took 71.4 seconds / 72 serial EntityData requests without rate limiting. Fourteen had Portico-matchable IDs. All 15 had Wikipedia sitelinks, although one did not resolve to a usable page; the source pass found 14 revision-pinned articles (12 English, 2 French) and one no-match after allowed Wikipedia searches. Eight of 15 had a canonical earliest year different from the raw query year.
- A v2 exploratory Luna batch on 10 above-threshold show/anime works took 53.5 seconds and emitted 79 structurally valid tags, but it is **rejected for enrichment**. A review found unsupported theme tags and a cross-title evidence error: the `The Continental` row claimed a corrupt autocratic ruler and a plan against him, which its own packet does not describe. The output also gave zero similar suggestions for well-known titles. Do not use its throughput as a safe scaled batch size; retest smaller batches under v3.
- A separate source-rule scan found forbidden review-site names in four Wikipedia packet excerpts, including Rotten Tomatoes and Metacritic. No forbidden site was fetched, but those excerpts should not have entered Luna prompts. The source extractor is being changed to remove those passages and regenerate fingerprints. Existing affected Luna outputs are not eligible for canonical commit.
- A read-only, five-suggestion Wikidata lookup canary (using rejected v2 suggestions solely to test mechanics) made 5 search and 11 entity requests in 15.5 seconds. Strict identity checks resolved `Onibaba`, `8½`, and `The Holy Mountain`; `The Impossible` and `The Witch` remained incomplete because the first search page capped at 50 hits. The accepted three QIDs have Wikidata provider IDs and sitelink counts above the provisional threshold. None was inserted into the pilot ledger.
- A bounded attempt to exercise new search continuation then received Wikidata `maxlag` on the first key, including its one retry. The lookup stopped and left all five unresolved for that run; no broad retries or ledger changes followed. Retry only after service lag clears, using the frozen cache.
