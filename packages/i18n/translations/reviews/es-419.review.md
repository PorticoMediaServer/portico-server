## Systematic problems

### 1. `vídeo` → `video` (26 messages, the headline miss)

The base `es` writes **`vídeo`** (peninsular: hiatus, esdrújula, *vi-de-o*). Latin American Spanish
writes **`video`** (llana, *vi-deo*, no accent) — this is the norm across Netflix, Disney+, Apple,
YouTube and Google in the region, and the RAE/Fundéu themselves describe it as the American form.

The translator's report claims the base es is "free of most classic Spainisms", but `vídeo` was
missed entirely: it is not in the glossary vocabulary table and none of the 26 occurrences were
overridden. It surfaces in the most visible parts of a media server — the player ("Vídeo",
"Convirtiendo vídeo", "formato de vídeo"), server settings ("Conversión de vídeo", "Carpeta para el
vídeo convertido", "Convertir el vídeo"), and capability/telemetry screens. This is the single
largest regionalization gap and is exactly the kind of peninsular marker `es-419` exists to remove.

Fix: bulk `vídeo` → `video` (`Vídeo` → `Video`, `Vídeos` → `Videos`, `vídeos` → `videos`), keeping
the gloss identical. All 26 units are in the review XLIFF.

### 2. `en emisión` → `al aire` (1 message)

The glossary maps *On now / On air* → **`Al aire`**, and the overlay applied it to `guide.onNow`,
`guide.onNowLeft`, `server.channels.state.ready` and `web.live.onNow`. One was missed:
`web.libraryChannels.onAir` ("This channel is on the air.") still reads **"Este canal está en
emisión."**, leaving the peninsular form beside its own localized siblings. → **"Este canal está al
aire."**

## Minor consistency findings

- **phone → `celular` (2):** the glossary maps *phone (device)* → `celular` and 10 strings use it,
  but `quickConnect.lede` ("…en un teléfono con sesión iniciada…") and the inherited
  `setup.app.notSetUp` ("…en un teléfono o tableta…") still say `teléfono`. Unify on `celular`.
- **Settings search empty state:** `settings.search.none` reads "Ningún **ajuste** coincide…" while
  the search field was renamed "Buscar **configuración**". → "Ninguna configuración coincide…".
- **`error.title.load` sign-in branch:** "No se ha podido **iniciar el inicio de sesión**" is
  repetitive ("start the sign-in"). → "No se ha podido **iniciar sesión**".

## Overall verdict

**Usable, but not fully regionalized.** The overlay's core decisions are sound and consistently
applied — `Configuración` (38), `TV en vivo`/`en vivo` (~45), `celular`/`computadora`,
`control remoto`, `Presiona`/`Mantén presionado` — with correct gender agreement and clean ICU.
There are no meaning errors or broken plurals, and the register is uniformly `tú`.

The one thing a Latin American user will still notice is the peninsular **`vídeo`** on 26 strings,
which the translator's "no Spainisms left" claim missed. Fix that (bulk find/replace), apply the
single `en emisión` → `al aire` correction, and tidy the four minor consistency items, and `es-419`
reads naturally for the region.
