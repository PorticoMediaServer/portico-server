# Glossary — Romanian (ro)

Machine translation of the Portico UI catalogue (October 2026), marked for
native-speaker review. Address form: **informal second person singular (tu)**
throughout — imperative verbs (Autentifică-te, Redă, Adaugă, Alege) and
possessives (serverul tău, contul tău) — consistent with how major streaming
apps address Romanian users.

## Typography and punctuation

- Diacritics always comma-below: **ș ț** (U+0219/U+021B), plus ă â î.
  Never cedilla ş/ţ.
- Quotation: Romanian low–high quotes **„…”** for quoted titles/names,
  matching the English curly quotes (never ASCII `"` or `'`).
- Ellipsis **…** (U+2026) kept where the English trails off (menu items that
  open a dialog: Descarcă…, Șterge…).
- No ASCII apostrophe anywhere (ICU quoting): hyphenated forms without
  apostrophe (n-a, s-a, ți-am, n-are) use the hyphen, which is correct
  Romanian spelling.
- Arrow separator **›** kept as in English (Portico › Setări).
- Product names never translated: **Portico, Portico Account, Portico
  Services**, FFmpeg, TMDB, TheTVDB, MusicBrainz, AniList, IMDb, mpv,
  OpenSubtitles, Google Cast, AirPlay, Dolby Vision, HDR, Plex-style feature
  names kept in English where they are brands. “Portico Accounts” (plural in
  English) is rendered as **Portico Account** because the tooling requires
  the literal product name.
- Language names (`language.*`) translated into Romanian, capitalised as in
  the other catalogues (Engleză, Franceză, … Română, …).

## Judgement calls for the reviewer

- **Tu, not dumneavoastră.** If a formal tone is preferred for account/security
  copy, only those strings need changing; UI verbs stay informal.
- **“Convertire” vs “conversie”** for transcode: “conversie” used everywhere
  (convertit, se convertește). “Transcodare” is also correct but rarer in
  consumer apps.
- **“Flux”** for stream (technical/reporting strings). Consumer-facing
  playback copy mostly avoids the word.
- **“Redare” vs “vizionare”**: Redare = the act of playing (player,
  sessions, errors); Vizionare = the experience (Continuă vizionarea,
  Vizionare împreună, Istoric de vizionare).
- **“Titlu”** for title (media item) throughout; “Nume” only for names of
  people/profiles/devices.
- **Gender-neutral impersonal forms** where English uses “you/your” with an
  unknown noun gender (se încarcă, s-a salvat, nu s-a putut). Possessives
  (tău/ta) kept where the noun gender is fixed.
- **Plurals**: every ICU plural carries one/few/other (0 → few, e.g.
  “0 filme”, “2 filme”, “19 filme”, “100 filme”). Exact `=0` branches from
  English are preserved alongside the three categories.
- **Long server-console sentences** (remote access, storage, FFmpeg,
  capabilities) favour clarity over brevity; UI labels stay close to the
  English length.
- Known weak spots: legal/licence strings (translated plainly, not by a
  lawyer); a few “Loading {thing}” fragments (traduse
  ca “Se încarcă …”); HDMI-CEC-style vendor words (HDHomeRun, Xtream,
  XMLTV, M3U) left in English.
