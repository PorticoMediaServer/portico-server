# Glossary — zh-Hans (Simplified Chinese, mainland)

Locale: zh-Hans. Register: plain, friendly written Chinese for UI copy, close to the house style of
mainland streaming apps (Apple TV, Netflix, Plex in Chinese). Mainland vocabulary throughout:
视频, 设置, 登录, 网络, 默认, 信息, 软件, 文件. Taiwanese forms (影片, 設定, 登入, 網路, 預設,
檔案, 伺服器, 帳號, 影集, 錄影) are never used. Traditional records were read for meaning only.

Address: instructions are impersonal where the sentence works without a pronoun (请重试, 请检查…).
你 is used where possession or direct address is needed (你的服务器, 你的账户). No 您.

Product names are never translated and stay in Latin script, as the validator requires:
Portico, Portico Account, Portico Services. Other brands and formats stay as they are: Apple TV,
iPhone, Android TV, Fire TV, Google Cast, AirPlay, TMDB, TheTVDB, AniList, MusicBrainz, OpenSubtitles,
HDHomeRun, DVR, MPV/mpv, FFmpeg, WebDAV, rclone, Dolby Vision (杜比视界 in running text), HDR.

## Punctuation and typography

- Full-width punctuation in prose: ，。！？：；“”（quoted titles use curly double quotes, not 「」）.
- Ellipsis: a menu item or action that opens a dialog ends in a single … (修改密码…, 删除账户…). Progress and waiting text ends in two-character …… (正在加载……).
- Percent is full-width ％ in prose ({value}％ and 音量 {percent}％). Half-width is kept for units
  and technical strings: Mbps, kbps, fps, p (for 1080p), 4K, HDR, ×.
- A single space separates a Latin word or number from adjacent Chinese text: Portico 会, TMDB 读取令牌,
  Apple TV 上的……. Numbers stay half-width.
- Ranges keep the en dash: {min}–{max}. Dates keep ISO form (2024-05-17) where the English does.
- No ASCII apostrophe anywhere (ICU quoting). Possessives are rephrased (…的账户, 你的服务器).
- Breadcrumbs keep the source ›: 设置 › 远程访问 › 安全连接.
- Leading and trailing spaces match the English exactly (validator-enforced).

## Plurals and selects

- zh-Hans has one plural category: `other`. Every plural uses `other` only, with `#` for the count.
  An exact `=0` branch is kept where the English has one (recordings.matches, cascadeProgress).
- Nested select keys are kept exactly as the English (lib.count pivot list, title.kind, web.metadata.history,
  player.skip). Measure words are chosen per noun inside the select (部电影, 部剧集, 位艺人, 张专辑,
  首歌曲, 本书, 个合集, 集, 位作者, 个类型, 个系列, 个标题).

## Judgement calls a reviewer should know

- **Language names are endonyms.** The preference list shows each language in its own name (English,
  日本語, Français, Deutsch, 中文 …), as the section's own note asks. The fr, de and zh-Hant records
  translate these names into the target language instead. A reviewer should decide which convention
  applies.
- **Show → 剧集, not 电视剧.** 电视剧 means a TV drama series in mainland usage and is too narrow for
  anime, documentaries and talk shows.
- **Stream → 视频流** when it is a noun ("the stream could not be loaded"), 播放 when it is the act of
  playback. "Another stream" → 另一个视频流.
- **Direct stream → 直接串流; Repackage → 重新封装** (remux without re-encoding).
- **Trickplay** → 进度条图片 (per the lane note: pictures made for the seek bar) and 预览缩略图 in the
  storage and job lists.
- **Watch Together 群组.** 群组 is used for the group of people, and 群组队列 for its queue. Watch with
  group → 与群组一起观看.
- **Channels vs Library Channels.** Live channels are 频道. Library Channels is 媒体库频道 (the custom
  name); the badge reads 频道.
- **Dolby Vision** → 杜比视界, the mainland name, in running text.
- **Restore → 还原** for backups. **恢复** is used for getting access back (恢复代码, 恢复所有者).
- **Mainland vs Taiwan Chinese terms in one place:** where a word differs (转码 vs 转檔, 下载 vs 下載,
  账户 vs 帳號), the mainland word is used throughout.
- **Labels for signed-in devices:** 已登录 for Signed in, 已退出登录 for Signed out.
- **Quotes around settings names** use “” (for example “使用其他账户或服务器”).
- **Recorded time left:** 剩余 is used for "left" in durations and progress ("剩余 30 分钟").
